Privacy and cookie policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

English · Versione italiana

In short

  • We use your data to assess your application, to organise the programme and our online info sessions, and to stay in touch only if you ask us to.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not use it for profiling and we do not take automated decisions about you.
  • This website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your data: write to info@iteralab.eu.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is MLBC Consulting Srl, Avenue Montjoie 13, 1180 Uccle, Brussels (Belgium), company and VAT number BE 0667781553, which runs the IteraLab programmes. For any question about your personal data, write to info@iteralab.eu.

IteraLab is in the process of setting up its own company. Once it is incorporated, that company will take over as controller of the data described in this notice. We will update this notice and tell people whose data we hold by email when this happens.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as the law does not require one for our activities.

2. What this notice covers

  • the application form for the IteraLab programmes;
  • the registration form for our online info sessions (the first one is on 2 December 2026);
  • registrations for our events on LinkedIn;
  • emails you send us;
  • your visit to this website, www.iteralab.eu.

3. What data we collect

Application form

The form is provided by Tally and collects: first and last name, email address, phone number, nationality, country and city of residence, LinkedIn profile (optional), current status, university or place of work, field of study, degree and expected graduation year, relevant experience, preferred edition, your level of Italian and English and other languages, professional areas of interest, any previous experience of Brussels, how you heard about IteraLab, your CV, your signatures confirming that you have read the information on the fee and on data protection, and your choices on being contacted and on receiving news of future programmes.

If you apply for an IteraLab Scholarship, we also collect your declaration that your household ISEE does not exceed € 30,000 and your motivation letter. If you are shortlisted, we ask for your ISEE certificate (or equivalent financial documentation for income or assets abroad).

Info session registration form

The form is provided by Tally and collects your first name, email address, what you are doing now (for example student or young professional) and your consent to receive the session link. If you reach the form from one of our posts or ads, the link also tells us which campaign you came from.

LinkedIn events

If you register for one of our events on LinkedIn, LinkedIn shares with us the information its event registration provides, such as your name, your LinkedIn profile and, where you agree to it, your email address.

Photographs and video during the programme

If you are admitted, we may take photographs and video during the edition. General and group shots, in which no one is the main subject (the room, the group, people seen from a distance), may be used to promote IteraLab on the basis of our legitimate interest in showing the programme. We tell participants on the first day, and you can object at any time: we will then avoid shots in which you can be recognised and remove those already published from the channels we control.

Close-ups and video in which you are clearly recognisable and the main subject, such as a portrait or an interview, are used only if you tick the optional consent box in the application form. You can withdraw that consent at any time, and you take part exactly as everyone else either way.

Emails

The messages you send us and our replies.

Your visit to the website

Like any website, ours is delivered by a hosting provider whose servers record technical data such as your IP address, your browser and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure. See also section 10 on cookies.

We do not ask for special categories of data, such as data about health or religious or political beliefs. Please do not include them in your CV or motivation letter.

5. Who receives your data

Your data is seen only by the IteraLab team who need it. We never sell it. Speakers do not receive the list of applicants or their CVs; if you want to be put in touch with a speaker, we do so only at your request.

We use these service providers, which process data on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Tally (Tally BV, Belgium): our application and registration forms;
  • Google (Google Workspace, provided by Google Ireland Ltd): email, file storage and the Google Meet video calls used for the info sessions;
  • Vercel (Vercel Inc., United States): hosting of this website;
  • for admitted participants, our accountant and our bank.

Some platforms act as independent controllers under their own privacy policies:

  • LinkedIn (LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company), for our page and our events on LinkedIn;
  • Meta (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd), when you interact with our pages or ads on Instagram and Facebook. We do not install the Meta pixel on this website;
  • Google, for the web fonts of this website and, if you choose to play it, the YouTube video.

6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Vercel and Google may process data in the United States. Where this happens, the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. Tally stores data in the European Union.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy (access);
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data (rectification);
  • delete your data, where we are not required to keep it (erasure);
  • limit or stop a processing activity, in particular one based on our legitimate interest (restriction and objection);
  • send you the data you gave us in a readable, reusable format (portability);
  • stop using your data for anything you consented to. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before.

Write to info@iteralab.eu. We reply within one month. If a request is complex we may need up to two further months, and we will tell you within the first month.

9. Complaints

If you think we are processing your data unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit), Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be, since our company is established in Belgium. You can also contact the authority of the EU country where you live or work; in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

We use no automated decision-making or profiling: every application is read and assessed by people.

10. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or similar tools for analytics, advertising or profiling, and it contains no tracking pixels (no Meta pixel, no LinkedIn Insight Tag). That is why you do not see a cookie banner.

Three services we use can still interact with your browser:

  • Application form. The form on the application page is provided by Tally and is embedded in the page. Tally may store technical information in your browser that the form needs to work.
  • Video. Our video is hosted on YouTube. It loads only when you press play, through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). From that moment YouTube may store information on your device under Google's privacy policy.
  • Fonts. The fonts of this website are loaded from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google receives your IP address; no cookies are set.

If we ever add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this notice and ask for your consent before using them.

11. Changes to this notice

We publish any new version on this page with its date. If a change affects the purposes or legal bases of processing, we will also tell the people concerned by email.

Tre servizi che usiamo possono comunque interagire con il tuo browser:

Se un giorno aggiungeremo strumenti di statistica o pubblicità, aggiorneremo questa informativa e ti chiederemo il consenso prima di usarli.
